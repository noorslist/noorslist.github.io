CG-USA invites you for a meal and meaningful conversation with neighbors who may see the world a little differently than you do.
Join Common Ground USA for a family-friendly night of community building where we'll get to know each other over good food. Bring an open mind and sense of curiousity as we share a meal with fellow Central Floridians. CG-USA staff and volunteers will help facilitate discussions about our values and beliefs, in hopes that we find we have more in common than we might think. We will also have an opportunity donate a canned good at Hope Helps food pantry. Let's get together, get to know each other, and give back to our community! This is a free event and we welcome you to bring your favorite dessert (and story!) to share.
