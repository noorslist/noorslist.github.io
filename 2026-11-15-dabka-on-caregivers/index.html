National Family Caregiver Month: Keep Calm, and Dabka On!

Celebrate National Family Caregiver Month with movement, joy, and community 💃🏽🕊️

“Keep Calm, and Dabka On” honors the quiet strength of caregivers while giving them space to breathe, laugh, and reconnect with their bodies and hearts. Join us for a presentation on Caregiver Support followed by a Live Dabke Dance Class by Micheline Maalouf of HabibyFit!

Ladies ages 10 and up are welcome—Muslim and non-Muslim Ladies, new to dabka or experienced dancers. By joining us, you support Bismillah Institute’s mission to remove barriers to quality mental health care for underserved, uninsured, and underinsured families. We do have a maximum capacity and so only ticketed guests will be given entry. Due to the fire code, children also require a ticket. Please note we will start at 11 am SHARP!
