Reminder · Daylight Saving Time ends

Clocks Fall Back 1 Hour

When
Sunday, November 1 · 2:00 AM

Daylight Saving Time ends on Sunday, November 1, 2026 at 2:00 a.m. local time, when clocks "fall back" one hour to 1:00 a.m.

Prayer times move an hour earlier on the clock, so check your masjid's updated iqamah times.
